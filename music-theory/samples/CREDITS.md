# Instrument sample credits

The recordings in this directory come from
[tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments) by
Nicholaus P. Brosowsky (code MIT; the sample packages it publishes list the
samples as CC-BY 3.0). They were thinned to about one note per minor third,
leveled, trimmed to 4 seconds and re-encoded with
`tools/music-theory-samples/build_samples.py`.

Original sources, per tonejs-instruments' `sample-source-info.txt`:

| Instrument | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Electric bass, electric guitar, saxophone | [Karoryfer Samples](https://www.karoryfer.com/karoryfer-samples) |
| Acoustic guitar | [University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios](http://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/) |
| Cello | Freesound pack 12408, "real cello notes" by flcellogrl |
| Nylon guitar | Freesound pack 11573, "classical guitar multisampled" by quartertone |
| Harmonium | Freesound pack 330410, "harmonium samples" by donyaquick |
| Bassoon, double bass, flute, French horn, harp, organ, trombone, trumpet, tuba, violin, xylophone | [Versilian Studios VSCO 2 Community Edition](http://vis.versilstudios.net/vsco-community.html) |

The piano is the Salamander Grand Piano, loaded from
`tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/`.
