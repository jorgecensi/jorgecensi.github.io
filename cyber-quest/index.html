    🎮 How to play

    Cyber Quest teaches the everyday security skills that stop most real attacks.

    📖 Story: Defend Brightside

    • You're the new security analyst at a small game studio, two weeks before its big launch. A hacker crew wants it. Five chapters, one per day of launch week.
    • Each chapter starts calm: your mentor Mira teaches you, you practise in a quiet terminal, and you answer a few questions with no clock.
    • Then the alarm goes off and the attack happens live: logs stream in, a threat meter climbs, and you fight back with what you just learned. If the meter hits 100%, you see what went wrong and try again.
    • Your story is saved after every step, so you can stop at any time.

    Missions

    • Each mission is a set of real-world situations: an email, a link, a phone call, a choice.
    • Decide what you'd do. After every answer you see why — read it, that's where the learning is.
    • Clear a mission to unlock the next one and add its rules to your Field Guide.

    Three tiers

    • 🟢 Beginner: phishing, links, passwords, scams and safe habits. No experience needed.
    • 🟡 Intermediate: email headers (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), ports and networks, cryptography, malware.
    • 🔴 Advanced: web vulnerabilities in real code, threat hunting in logs, security architecture and incident response.
    • Beat a tier's boss to unlock the next tier. Harder tiers are worth more XP per answer.
    • Later tiers add new challenges: tap the vulnerable line of code or the suspicious log entry, and put steps in the right order.

    Shields & stars

    • You start each mission with 🛡️🛡️🛡️. A wrong answer costs one. Lose all three and you're breached — try again.
    • No mistakes = ⭐⭐⭐. One mistake = ⭐⭐. More = ⭐.
    • XP counts your best run on each mission, and your rank grows with it.

    Bosses

    • Each tier ends with a boss: ten random questions from that tier, against the clock (20, 25 and 30 seconds per question).

    💻 Terminal Ops

    • A simulated Linux server you defend with real commands — ls, cat, grep, ps, kill, chmod, ss, ufw, systemctl and more. You can even pipe, like cat file | grep word.
    • 📘 Basics start from zero: moving around, where Linux keeps settings and logs, reading logs, processes and scheduled jobs, permissions and services. Each step has a short 📖 lesson above the terminal that explains the idea and the command to try.
    • 🚨 Incidents unlock after the Basics: you use what you learned to catch an intruder and harden the server, with lessons that point back to the Basics.
    • Stuck? Type hint for the exact command, help for every command, or man ls to explain one.
    • It's a sandbox in your browser on a fake filesystem — nothing touches a real machine. Labs unlock in order and earn XP like missions.

    Privacy

    • Everything runs on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
    • Still: never type a password you really use into any game or quiz — that's lesson one!
