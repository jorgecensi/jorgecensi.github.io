    🎮 How to play

    Cyber Quest teaches the everyday security skills that stop most real attacks.

    Missions

    • Each mission is a set of real-world situations: an email, a link, a phone call, a choice.
    • Decide what you'd do. After every answer you see why — read it, that's where the learning is.
    • Clear a mission to unlock the next one and add its rules to your Field Guide.

    Shields & stars

    • You start each mission with 🛡️🛡️🛡️. A wrong answer costs one. Lose all three and you're breached — try again.
    • No mistakes = ⭐⭐⭐. One mistake = ⭐⭐. More = ⭐.
    • XP counts your best run on each mission, and your rank grows with it.

    The final boss

    • Clear every mission to unlock Breach Day: ten mixed questions against the clock.

    Privacy

    • Everything runs on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
    • Still: never type a password you really use into any game or quiz — that's lesson one!
